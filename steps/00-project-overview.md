# Step 0: Project Overview — AeroTrack

## What is this project?

**AeroTrack** is a smart contract that tracks airplane spare parts on the Ethereum blockchain. Think of it like a digital passport for every airplane part — recording who made it, who owns it, and every maintenance check it goes through.

## Why blockchain for airplane parts?

Imagine you buy a used car. You want to see its full service history to make sure it wasn't in an accident. Now imagine the same thing, but for a **turbine blade on a Boeing 737**. If someone fakes the maintenance records, people could die.

**Blockchain solves this** because:
- Nobody can delete or modify past records (immutable)
- Everyone can verify the data (transparent)
- No single company controls the database (decentralized)
